Your Safety is Our Priority

Talkory is built so you can talk freely and stay protected. Here is how Talkory Tech keeps conversations safe, and what you can do to help.

Safety Features

Important safety noticeTalkory is a place to talk and be heard. It is not a substitute for emergency services or professional help. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services right away.
🚫

Blocking

Block anyone straight from the chat screen or the screen after a call. A blocked person cannot contact you again.

🚩

Reporting

Report a chat or a call in a few taps. Our team reviews every report and can warn, suspend or remove accounts.

💬

Chat Safety Filter

Abusive and illegal words, phone numbers and other contact details are stopped automatically. Repeated breaches lead to a suspension.

🛡️

Verified Hosts

Every host completes identity (KYC) verification and signs the host agreement before going online.

🖼️

Photo and Intro Review

Host photos and voice intros are checked, including an automatic AI-image check, and approved by our team before anyone sees them.

🔒

Privacy Protection

You stay anonymous. Your phone number is never shown to hosts, and hosts' numbers are never shown to you.

🎧

Support Tickets

Raise a problem with Support from inside the app and follow the reply. We acknowledge within 24 hours.

⏸️

You Are in Control

End a call or leave a chat at any time. Hosts can go Busy or Offline whenever they want.

Staying safe on Talkory

• Never share your phone number, social media, bank or UPI details, passwords or OTPs. Talkory will never ask for them.

• Do not send money or gifts outside Talkory. Anyone who asks you to is breaking our rules: report them.

• Do not record calls or chats or share them with others.

• If something feels wrong, block and report. You do not need to explain yourself.

Need help or want to complain?

Open Support in the app, or write to talkorytech@gmail.com. Our Grievance Officer's details are in the Privacy Policy.
